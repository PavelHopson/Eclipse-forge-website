# Eclipse Forge OS positioning audit — Stage 0 draft

## Executive decision

Eclipse Forge has a credible technical foundation for a differentiated position, but
the public message is currently broader than the proof and buying path. The strongest
initial wedge is not “all AI products in one mega service.” It is one controlled job:

> Turn a public product question into an evidence-backed decision using a bounded AI
> team, with visible cost, permissions and human approval.

This is a working positioning hypothesis, not evidence of market demand.

## Audit card

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Eclipse Forge OS |
| Primary audience | Technical founder or product owner in a small team |
| High-cost problem | AI research and content work is difficult to trust, govern and connect to a business decision |
| First job | Produce a cited positioning/opportunity audit from public sources |
| Funnel | consideration -> conversion |
| First offer | AI Opportunity Audit |
| Primary CTA | Request an AI Opportunity Audit |
| Proof mechanism | Observable run, evidence cards, rejected claims, bounded budget and human decision |
| Status | Draft ready for human review; not approved for publication |

## Current strengths

1. **The ecosystem has explicit product roles.** The public manifest distinguishes
   Landing, Chat, Library, AI Hub, Sentinel and Media rather than hiding them behind a
   generic “AI platform” label (`evidence-ecosystem-manifest`).
2. **The control thesis is coherent.** Current copy consistently emphasizes systems,
   permissions, tools, guardrails and operator workflows (`evidence-landing-canonical`,
   `evidence-landing-repository`).
3. **There is public engineering depth to inspect.** The brand links to multiple public
   repositories and a named engineering identity (`evidence-public-profile`).
4. **Maturity can be represented honestly.** The manifest already separates `live`,
   `beta`, `prototype` and `library`, preventing a false all-production narrative.

## Positioning gaps

| Gap | Why it blocks conversion | Recommended correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Category breadth | “AI, automation, SaaS, UX” describes capabilities but not the first buyer decision | Lead with the controlled AI work outcome; keep capabilities as support |
| Portfolio-first navigation | Many projects require the buyer to assemble the value proposition | Route the primary journey through one Office, one offer and one CTA |
| Proof unevenness | Repository existence does not prove production usage or business outcome | Show an accepted observable run and preserve maturity labels |
| Unverified metric claims | Copy such as “6 production systems running 24/7” requires reproducible operational evidence | Downgrade or attach a dated public verification record before reuse |
| Offer ambiguity | Custom systems, audits and products compete for the same next click | Make AI Opportunity Audit the first bounded commercial step |
| Measurement absence | A contact click cannot yet be attributed to audience, message or asset | Add privacy-reviewed events and UTM v1 before the first published experiment |

## Recommended message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** get an evidence-backed decision, not a pile of AI output.
2. **Control:** see roles, sources, cost, state and pending approvals.
3. **Boundary:** agents cannot publish or mutate external systems without an exact human
   decision.
4. **Proof:** inspect one real positioning-audit run and the claims it rejected.
5. **Offer:** request a bounded AI Opportunity Audit.

Working proposition:

> Eclipse Forge OS turns a business objective into an observable run by a bounded AI
> team. Every material claim keeps its evidence, every external action requires exact
> human approval, and every accepted artifact has a measurable next step.

Short version for testing:

> Controlled AI teams for evidence-backed product decisions.

## Claim policy for the first funnel

### Allowed now

- Eclipse Forge is a public multi-product engineering ecosystem.
- Eclipse Forge OS is the flagship internal product program.
- The first Growth workflow is designed around bounded roles, evidence and human review.
- Some products are live, while others are beta or prototype, exactly as the manifest
  labels them.

### Requires more evidence

- end-to-end reliability, cancellation success and exact cost;
- time saved or editing-time reduction;
- accepted artifact rate and repeat use;
- production uptime or number of continuously running systems;
- buyer preference for the proposed category and CTA.

### Prohibited without new evidence

- customer, revenue, testimonial, partnership or PMF claims;
- guaranteed income, conversion or replacement of a specialist;
- “fully autonomous,” “zero risk,” or “all products production-ready”;
- comparisons whose competitor capabilities were not audited from current primary
  sources.

## First measurable experiment

Create one preview landing section for the AI Opportunity Audit after this draft is
accepted. Show the input, bounded process, review artifact, exclusions and one CTA.

Success is not post volume. The primary result is at least one qualified audit request
attributable to the section during the agreed measurement window. If there is traffic
but no qualified action, interview the visitors or change the proposition; do not scale
content production.

## Repurposing after acceptance

- sanitized observable-run demo;
- technical build-in-public article about evidence and approval design;
- Production Readiness checklist lead magnet;
- GitHub architecture note linking to the public contracts;
- one concise Telegram/Threads post pointing to the same measured CTA.

All variants reuse the same evidence decisions. None may introduce new result claims.
